## Shorter parameter names
In your DAW, Floe's parameter names are long and descriptive, such as "Effect Distortion On". If your DAW shows them in a narrow space, you can switch to abbreviated names such as "Dist On": turn on _Abbreviate parameter names in DAW_ on the _General_ tab of Preferences. You might need to restart your DAW for the change to take effect.
