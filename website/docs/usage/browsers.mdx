Floe's browsers are how you find and load sounds. There are three, and they all work the same way:
- The preset browser, opened from the preset box at the top of the window.
- An Instrument browser for each layer, opened from the layer's Instrument selector.
- The impulse response (IR) browser, opened from the IR selector in the Convolution Reverb effect.

## Overview

<GeneratedScreenshot name="browser-browse" alt="Layer 1's Instrument browser, open beneath its selector, with its regions numbered" />

1. **The box you opened the browser from.** It stays where it is and keeps working while the browser is open. It shows the current item, and its buttons still load the previous <FAIcon icon="fa-solid fa-caret-left" /> and next <FAIcon icon="fa-solid fa-caret-right" /> item from the results, load a random <FAIcon icon="fa-solid fa-shuffle" /> one, or unload the current one. The browser opens directly beneath it, or above it when there's no room below.
1. **Results.** Every item that matches what's selected on the right, grouped by folder. Click one to load it. The browser then closes by itself once you move the mouse away from it, so you can click through several sounds before you leave. Hover an item and click its <FAIcon icon="fa-regular fa-star" /> star to make it a favourite. The loaded item is highlighted, and the list scrolls to it when the browser opens.
1. **What you're looking at.** A sentence describing the results, such as _Showing all presets_, _Showing from Abyss_ or _Showing favourites matching "pad" with Tag: Ambient_. Each named part is a button: click its <FAIcon icon="fa-solid fa-xmark" /> to remove that filter, clear the search, or step back out of the library or preset bank.
1. **Results toolbar.** <FAIcon icon="fa-solid fa-location-arrow" /> jumps to the currently loaded item, clearing anything that hides it first. <FAIcon icon="fa-solid fa-star" /> shows only your favourites. <FAIcon icon="fa-solid fa-magnifying-glass" /> opens a search box for narrowing the results by name. Search and Favourites both refine whatever is currently shown: open a preset bank and turn on Favourites to see your favourites within that bank. If nothing matches within the current selection, the results offer a button to search everything instead.
1. **Where to look.** Libraries or preset banks, folders, tags and more. This panel has two modes, Browse and Filter, described below.
1. **Trail.** In Browse mode, where you are and how to get back: back and forward arrows, then a <FAIcon icon="fa-solid fa-house" /> house for the starting page and a crumb for each level below it. Click any crumb to jump back to it.
1. **Browse/Filter toggle.** <FAIcon icon="fa-solid fa-list" /> switches the right panel to Browse mode and <FAIcon icon="fa-solid fa-filter" /> to Filter mode. Floe remembers which you last used.
1. **Resize.** Drag the corner to resize the browser. You can also drag the border between the two panels to change how much room each gets. Double-click either to reset it.

Numbers in brackets, such as _(26)_, show how many items a library, folder or tag holds.

To close a browser, click anywhere outside it or press Escape.

## Browse mode

Browse mode, the default, shows one thing at a time. You start from a short list of places to look and drill down until the results are just what you want. There's nothing to configure and it's hard to lose your place.

The starting page has two groups. _Browse by collection_ lists your libraries, or in the preset browser your preset banks. _Browse by attribute_ lists other ways of looking at your sounds, such as tags and authors.

Open _Libraries_ and every library is listed, one per row.

<GeneratedScreenshot name="browser-browse-section" alt="Browse mode listing every library" />

Click a library and the results show only its Instruments, while the right panel shows the library's folders.

<GeneratedScreenshot name="browser-browse-collection" alt="Browse mode showing one library and its folders" />

Click a folder to show just its items. Click it again, or click the <FAIcon icon="fa-solid fa-xmark" /> beside its name in the summary line, to go back to the whole library.

The attribute pages work the same way. _Tags_ lists the tag categories first, such as _Mood_ or _Sound source_; open a category and it lists its tags. Click a tag and the results show every item with that tag, from all your libraries. The page stays open, so you can try the next tag straight away.

<GeneratedScreenshot name="browser-browse-attribute" alt="Browse mode listing the tag categories" />

Browse mode always shows one library, folder or tag at a time. To combine several, such as a tag within one library, switch to Filter mode with the toggle at the bottom of the right panel.

## Filter mode

Filter mode shows every library, folder and tag at once, as a tree. Each is a filter that narrows the results, and you can select several. It works like shopping online: pick a size, a colour and a price range, then browse what's left.

<GeneratedScreenshot name="browser-full" alt="Filter mode with a library and a tag selected, with its regions numbered" />

1. **The filter tree.** Libraries and preset banks are collections with their folders inside; tags, authors and other categories are plain buttons. Click a heading to expand or collapse a section. Because everything is on screen at once, the toolbar has its own <FAIcon icon="fa-solid fa-magnifying-glass" /> search for finding a filter by name.
1. **Selected filters.** Each one appears in the summary line, joined by _and_ or _or_. Click a filter's <FAIcon icon="fa-solid fa-xmark" /> to remove it.
1. **Browse/Filter toggle.** Switch back to Browse mode from here. Only one of the selected filters survives the switch, since Browse mode shows one thing at a time.
1. **How filters combine.** Click to choose:
   - _One_: only one filter can be selected at a time. Clicking a filter replaces the current selection.
   - _Match all (AND)_: an item must match every selected filter. The more you select, the narrower the results. Floe greys out filters that would leave nothing, so you don't end up with dead-end combinations.
   - _Match any (OR)_: an item can match any of the selected filters. The more you select, the broader the results.

### Collections

Libraries, in the Instrument and IR browsers, and preset banks, in the preset browser, are collections. A collection has a header (**1**) and a body (**2**).

<GeneratedScreenshot name="filter-collection" alt="A library collection with its header and body numbered" />

Click the header to expand or collapse the collection. Inside, the _All_ row selects everything in the library or preset bank, and the rows below it select individual folders. A selected row is highlighted.

<GeneratedScreenshot name="filter-collection-all-selected" alt="A library collection with All selected" />
<GeneratedScreenshot name="filter-collection-folder-selected" alt="A library collection with one folder selected" />

Folders can be nested. When you select a folder, its subfolders are included too, and the lines connecting them turn gold to show it. Here _Mic Options_ is selected, so _Close_ and _Room_ are part of the results.

<GeneratedScreenshot name="filter-collection-folder-tree" alt="A library collection with a parent folder selected, its subfolders joined by gold lines" />

A folder can hold items of its own as well as subfolders, just like folders on your computer, so selecting a parent can show items that aren't in any of its subfolders.

## The preset browser

<GeneratedScreenshot name="browser-preset-browse" alt="The preset browser open beneath the preset box, on its starting page" />

The preset browser has a few extras of its own:
- When you have both kinds, preset banks are split into _Factory preset banks_, which came with your libraries, and _User presets_, which you made or installed yourself.
- _Preset authors_, and _Preset type_ when you have presets in more than one format, are extra ways to browse.
- _Libraries used_ lists the libraries that presets take their sounds from, so you can find every preset that uses a particular library.
- Search matches preset names only, not the names of preset banks or folders.
- Right-click a preset for _Open Containing Folder_, _Set as Default Preset_, and sending the preset to the trash.

## Right-click

Right-click a library or a preset bank, in either mode, to open its folder on your computer or to uninstall it. Folders within a preset bank offer the same.

## Keyboard navigation

The browsers support keyboard navigation using the arrow keys and enter.

The focused item is marked by a blue outline. The outline is hidden until you press an arrow key.

- Up/down arrows to move the focus in the results list.
- Enter/return to 'click' the focused item.
- Tab to move between the results list and the right panel.
- Left/right arrows to move along the breadcrumb at the bottom of the right panel.
- CTRL+F (CMD+F on Mac) to focus the results search box.
- Home/End to jump to the start/end of the list.
- Hold CTRL (CMD on Mac) and use the up/down arrows to jump to the previous/next folder.
- Page Up/Page Down to jump by 8 items at a time.

Libraries, preset banks and folders are keyboard-navigable; tag and other filter buttons currently aren't.

## Tips
- The previous, next and random buttons beside the box that opens a browser follow what the browser is showing. Open a library or select a tag, close the browser, and step through just those sounds.
- Right-click a filter's button or row for more options; in the results, right-click is available on presets.
