Floe 2.0.4 is out now - a big update with redesigned browsers, a reworked Distortion effect, a new Limiter, shared grains and lots of improvements throughout.

{/* truncate */}

![Floe v2.0.4 preset browser in Browse mode](/images/blog/2026-10-20-floe-2-0-4-browser.png)

## Browsers

The preset, Instrument and IR browsers have had another rework, shaped by your feedback. With several libraries installed, finding things took a lot of scrolling, and most of the time you don't want to combine filters - you just want to pick one thing and see what's in it.

The new Browse mode is much cleaner and feels more like a menu, opening right beneath the selector you clicked. It gets you to a library, preset bank, folder or tag in fewer clicks. The browsers can be resized too. Filter mode is still there if you prefer the previous way of working. See the [browsers docs](/docs/usage/browsers) for a full walkthrough.

## Distortion and Limiter

We've improved the [Distortion](/docs/usage/effects#distortion) effect to be richer and avoid volume changes as you turn up the drive. There are 10 new or upgraded types, a cleaner sound with 4x oversampling, new Punish and Tilt controls, and Auto Gain. Additionally, a new display shows you the real distortion shape. Existing presets and DAW projects load with the older types, kept as Legacy types.

![Distortion effect with shape preview](/images/blog/2026-10-20-floe-2-0-4-distortion.png)

There's also a new [Limiter](/docs/usage/effects#limiter) effect, mainly for the end of the effects chain, with input, gain-reduction and output meters.

![Limiter effect with meters](/images/blog/2026-10-20-floe-2-0-4-limiter.png)

## Shared grains

Granular layers have a new [Share Grains](/docs/usage/playback-tab#share-grains) option. With shared grains, the number of grains stays the same however many notes you play. So a sparse, delicate granular texture keeps its sparseness and intricate character even when you play wide chords, rather than building up into a dense wash with every note you add. It's lovely for creating fragile, detailed textures.

![Share Grains button tooltip on a granular layer](/images/blog/2026-10-20-floe-2-0-4-shared-grains.png)

## Redesigned effects rack

The Effects page has an improved switchboard where you can now click an effect to make the rack [jump straight to it](/docs/usage/effects#adding-and-removing-effects) - really handy for finding your way around once you've got a few effects going.

![Effects page with the effects switches and FX rack](/images/blog/2026-10-20-floe-2-0-4-effects.png)

## Other highlights

- **Faster audio engine**: up to 2.5x faster in heavy granular use
- **Clearer macros**: displays and value popups show what macros are doing, and [Edit Range](/docs/usage/macros#editing-the-range) lets you set a destination's range while hearing each end
- **[Variation](/docs/usage/playback-tab#variation)** for granular layers and random LFO shapes, so a preset can repeat the exact same grain scatter or LFO movement on every note or on each key
- **[Default preset](/docs/presets/loading-presets#default-preset)**: choose what new instances of Floe open with
- More descriptive tooltips, with values shown straight away on hover
- New displays and meters: a LUFS meter, peak-hold lines, Compressor meters, a High-pass filter graph and a Bit Crush shape preview
- Overhauled documentation
- Smoother, more useful Bit Crush controls
- Swap layers and macros from their right-click menus
- LFO Amount as an MPE press and slide target
- Wider layer Pitch range of ±88 semitones
- Respond to MIDI panic messages (All Notes Off and All Sound Off)

And loads more besides - see the full [changelog](/docs/changelog) for everything.

As always, Floe is free and open source. Grab v2.0.4 from the [download page](/download).
