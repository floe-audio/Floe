Libraries and presets are easiest to remove from inside Floe.

When Floe removes a library or preset bank, it sends it to your Trash or Recycle Bin rather than deleting it outright, so if you remove one by mistake, you can restore it from there.

You might be wondering why there's no mention of _packages_ on this page. Packages are just the means of installing content into Floe. Sample libraries and presets are what actually get installed, so they're what you uninstall.

## Uninstalling sample libraries

1. Open the <FAIcon icon="fa-solid fa-ellipsis-v" /> menu in the top panel and choose _Info_.
1. On the _Libraries_ tab, click _Uninstall_ on <ManualExclude types={['library']}>the library you want to remove</ManualExclude><ManualOnly types={['library']}><LibraryName />'s card</ManualOnly>, and confirm.

<GeneratedScreenshot name="uninstall-library" alt="A library's card in Floe's Info panel, with the Uninstall button" />

You can also right-click <LibraryName>a library's name</LibraryName> in the Instrument or IR browser and choose _Uninstall_.

## Uninstalling presets

1. Open the preset browser by clicking the preset's name in the top panel.
1. Right-click the preset bank in the right-hand panel and choose _Uninstall_, then confirm.

<GeneratedScreenshot name="uninstall-preset-bank" alt="The right-click menu of a preset bank in the preset browser" />

The same works for a single folder of presets.<ManualExclude types={['library']}> Repeat for each preset bank you want to remove.</ManualExclude>

## Uninstalling Floe

### <FAIcon icon="fa-brands fa-windows" /> Windows

Close your DAW, then uninstall Floe like any other program, from _Installed apps_ (or _Add or Remove Programs_) in Windows' settings, where it's listed as _Floe Audio Plugin_. See [Microsoft's guide](https://support.microsoft.com/en-gb/windows/uninstall-or-remove-apps-and-programs-in-windows-4b55f974-2cc6-2d2b-d092-5905080eaf98) if you need help.

This removes the Floe plugins, but not your libraries or presets.<ManualExclude types={['library']}> The uninstaller can also remove Mirage, Floe's predecessor.</ManualExclude>

### <FAIcon icon="fa-brands fa-apple" /> macOS and <FAIcon icon="fa-brands fa-linux" /> Linux

There's no uninstaller: delete Floe's files yourself. The plugin files are the important ones, but you can delete the rest of Floe's files too, if you want to be thorough. See [File Locations](./file-locations) for where they all are.
