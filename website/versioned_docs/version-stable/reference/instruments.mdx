An Instrument is a playable sound source. It might be a single sample that loops, or many samples mapped across the keyboard<LibraryFeature has="velocity-layers">, with velocity layers and round robins</LibraryFeature>.

You load an Instrument into any of the three [layers](../usage/layers) with the [Instrument browser](../usage/browsers)<ManualExclude types={['library']}>, which finds Instruments across all your installed sample libraries</ManualExclude>. A layer with no Instrument loaded shows _None_ in place of the name: click it to open the browser.

<ManualExclude types={['library']}>
Instruments come from sample libraries, which are made with Floe's open developer tools.
