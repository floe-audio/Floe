The _EQ_ tab has a three-band equaliser for shaping the layer's tone before it's mixed with the other layers.

<GeneratedScreenshot name="layer-eq" alt="A layer's EQ tab with its parts numbered" />

1. **On/off** — switch the EQ on or off. A dot on the _EQ_ tab shows that it's on.
1. **EQ graph** — shows the combined effect of all three bands. Drag a band's point to move it, use your mouse scroll wheel over it to change its resonance, or right-click it to change its type or copy and paste the whole band. For band types without a gain control, dragging up and down changes the resonance.
1. **Bands** — the three bands have identical controls:
   - **Type** — a peak or shelf for boosting and cutting, a notch for removing a narrow slice, or a pass filter for rolling off one end of the spectrum.
   - **Freq** — where the band does its work: the centre of a peak or notch, the corner of a shelf, or the cutoff of a pass filter.
   - **Reso** — how tightly the band is focused. For peaks and notches it's the width; shelves use it to steepen their corner, and pass filters to add a peak at the cutoff.
   - **Gain** — how far the band boosts or cuts. Only the peak and shelf types use it.
