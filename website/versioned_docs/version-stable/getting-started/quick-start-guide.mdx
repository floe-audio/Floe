## Before you start

Floe is an engine — empty without sample libraries. Libraries and their presets come in files called _Floe packages_. If you've bought a library, you'll usually download its package from the developer's website, or find a link in your email.

If you don't have any packages yet, browse the [packages](/packages) page to discover free and paid libraries.

</ManualExclude>

## Finding your way around

### Tooltips

If you hover over a control for a moment, a tooltip pops up explaining what it does. They go well beyond naming the control: each one tells you how it behaves and what's worth knowing about it. When you're not sure what something does, a tooltip is usually the quickest way to find out. If you'd rather not see them, turn off _Show tooltips_ in the <FAIcon icon="fa-solid fa-cog" /> preferences.

### Using the controls

Knobs and sliders all work the same way: drag up or right to increase the value, hold `shift` for finer control, and double-click to type an exact value. Right-click a control, or a whole section such as a layer's tab or an effect, for more options like copy and paste, resetting and MIDI CC Learn. See [Controls](../usage/controls) for the full details.

### Window size

To make the window bigger or smaller, drag its bottom-right corner. In some DAWs, such as Logic Pro, you need to grab this exact corner of <LibraryName>Floe</LibraryName> rather than the corner of the DAW's window around it.

### Some terminology

A few words come up often in this manual. We mean something specific when we use them:

- **Preset** — a complete, saved sound, with its own name, description and tags.
- **Layer** — one of the three sound layers that this engine revolves around. See them side by side on the LAYERS page.
- **Instrument** — the sound source that a layer plays. It might be a single sample or many samples spread across the keyboard.

### The three main pages

Most of your time in <LibraryName>Floe</LibraryName> is spent on its three main pages. In future chapters we'll cover them in full detail:

- [**Perform**](../usage/perform) — a distraction-free view for exploring presets and making quick changes. The easiest place to start with <LibraryName>Floe</LibraryName>.
- [**Layers**](../usage/layers) — the three layers and their Instruments, each with its detailed controls.
- [**Effects**](../usage/effects) — the effects rack that colours the whole sound.
