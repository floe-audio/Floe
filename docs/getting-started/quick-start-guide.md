## Before you start

Floe is an engine — empty without sample libraries. Libraries and their presets come in files called _Floe packages_. If you've bought a library, you'll usually download its package from the developer's website, or find a link in your email.

If you don't have any packages yet, browse the [packages](/packages) page to discover free and paid libraries.

## Install Floe and your packages

1.  **Download Floe** from [floe.audio/download](https://floe.audio/download) and run the installer for your computer. See [Install Floe](/docs/installation/download-and-install-floe) if you need more detail.
2.  **Download your packages.**
3.  **Open your DAW** and add Floe to an instrument track. It's usually listed as a virtual instrument under _Floe Audio_.
4.  **Open Floe's preferences** by clicking in the top panel, then choose the _Packages_ tab.
5.  **Click _Install package_** and select your package file. You can select several at once.

![The Packages tab of Floe's preferences, with the Install package button](https://assets.floe.audio/website/screenshots/install-packages.732780b46497022b.png)

Some packages, often paid ones, need unlocking: Floe asks for the license key that came with your purchase.

When the installation finishes, Floe shows a summary. Once you click _Done_, your libraries and presets are ready to use. There's no need to restart anything.

You can delete the package files afterwards; Floe doesn't need them anymore. If you want to install to a different drive, or want more details, have a look at [Install Packages](/docs/installation/install-packages).

## Play your first preset

If the plugin was empty when you installed the package, it will have already loaded one of the library's presets for you — begin playing some notes on your MIDI controller.

### Try some presets

![The top of Floe's window, with the PERFORM tab and the preset arrows numbered](https://assets.floe.audio/website/screenshots/perform-tab-and-arrows.08e76e54b4999528.png)12

1.  Click the PERFORM tab button near the top of Floe's UI.
2.  Use the large arrow buttons, and , to step through presets.

### Explore the presets

![The preset browser, open beneath the outlined preset box](https://assets.floe.audio/website/screenshots/preset-browser.661d250ce632b3cd.png)

1.  Open the preset browser by clicking the preset's name in the top panel.
2.  Click a preset in the list on the left to load it. Narrow down the list using the right panel.
3.  Close the browser by moving the mouse away from it, clicking outside it, or pressing `escape`.

## Finding your way around

### Tooltips

If you hover over a control for a moment, a tooltip pops up explaining what it does. They go well beyond naming the control: each one tells you how it behaves and what's worth knowing about it. When you're not sure what something does, a tooltip is usually the quickest way to find out. If you'd rather not see them, turn off _Show tooltips_ in the preferences.

### Using the controls

Knobs and sliders all work the same way: drag up or right to increase the value, hold `shift` for finer control, and double-click to type an exact value. Right-click a control, or a whole section such as a layer's tab or an effect, for more options like copy and paste, resetting and MIDI CC Learn. See [Controls](/docs/usage/controls) for the full details.

### Window size

To make the window bigger or smaller, drag its bottom-right corner. In some DAWs, such as Logic Pro, you need to grab this exact corner of Floe rather than the corner of the DAW's window around it.

### Some terminology

A few words come up often in this manual. We mean something specific when we use them:

-   **Preset** — a complete, saved sound, with its own name, description and tags.
-   **Layer** — one of the three sound layers that this engine revolves around. See them side by side on the LAYERS page.
-   **Instrument** — the sound source that a layer plays. It might be a single sample or many samples spread across the keyboard.

### The three main pages

Most of your time in Floe is spent on its three main pages. In future chapters we'll cover them in full detail:

-   [**Perform**](/docs/usage/perform) — a distraction-free view for exploring presets and making quick changes. The easiest place to start with Floe.
-   [**Layers**](/docs/usage/layers) — the three layers and their Instruments, each with its detailed controls.
-   [**Effects**](/docs/usage/effects) — the effects rack that colours the whole sound.
