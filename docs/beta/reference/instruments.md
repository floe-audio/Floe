# Instruments

> Instruments are Floe's core building block

An Instrument is a playable sound source. It might be a single sample that loops, or many samples mapped across the keyboard, with velocity layers and round robins.

You load an Instrument into any of the three [layers](/docs/beta/usage/layers) with the [Instrument browser](/docs/beta/usage/browsers), which finds Instruments across all your installed sample libraries. A layer with no Instrument loaded shows _None_ in place of the name: click it to open the browser.

Instruments come from sample libraries, which are made with Floe's open developer tools.
